# GemInsight

GemInsight is an AI-powered leadership cockpit for The Arckitects Pvt. Ltd., a retail company. It shows how company leaders will work in the future: all data in one place, a view for each leader, and AI that turns decisions into action.

Live app: https://geminsight.ai.studio/
Best viewed on a laptop or desktop.

## What it does
- Command Centre: View your organization from the top, any and all details that you need to know about the company health
- Intelligence Hub: a daily brief or news flash of your company 
- Risk & Opportunity Radar: a "focus area" for the CXO to spend his time on pressing strategies
- Action Cockpit: strategies split into tasks for AI agents and people to execute
- Talk to Data: ask questions regarding anything and everything

## Data sources that it would utilize for functioning
a. Internal Structured data (ERP, Operational systems, HR systems, Supply chain systems, etc.)
b. Internal Unstructured data (employee reports, google chat/MS teams, emails, sharepoints etc.)
c. External Approved Sources (authorized news channels, industry benchmark reports, government portals, network portals, etc.)

## Built with
Google AI Studio, Gemini, Firebase, Firestore, Cloud Run
